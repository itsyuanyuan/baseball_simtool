Season statistics

Batting sorts by HR; pitching by strikeouts. Rates include all players, with no minimum workload. Stats stay with the team where they were earned. “—” means no denominator or a counter unavailable in an older save. RA9 counts all runs, not earned runs. FIP* uses an illustrative fixed 3.10 constant; it is not season-adjusted.

WAR* is a simulator estimate, not official fWAR or bWAR. BsR* and Def Runs* now include tracked running and defensive opportunities. Fixed run weights are approximations; framing, park and relief-leverage adjustments are not modeled. Batting and pitching WAR are separate contributions; add them for a two-way player. Older or partially tracked seasons show “—” for full-season values; Fielding / running shows tracked games and opportunity counts.

Stat definitions & WAR method

SLG: total bases per at-bat. OPS: OBP + SLG. ISO: SLG − AVG. BABIP: (H − HR)/(AB − SO − HR + SF). WHIP: (H + BB)/IP. K% and BB% use PA for batters, batters faced for pitchers. K−BB%: strikeout rate minus walk rate. FIP*: [13 × HR + 3 × (BB + HBP) − 2 × SO]/IP + 3.10.

FIP definition · Batting definitions. Earned-run accounting and pitcher decisions are not yet tracked. Standard counting stats remain available in the Standard view.

WAR* uses the selected phase’s simulated league average and 10 runs per win. Batting: (batting runs above average + BsR* + Def Runs* + position runs + replacement runs)/10. Fixed wOBA* weights: BB .69, HBP .72, 1B .89, 2B 1.27, 3B 1.62, HR 2.10, divided by PA; batting runs = (player wOBA* − league wOBA*)/1.20 × PA. Stolen-base runs = .20 × SB − .40 × CS, minus the league rate × PA. Replacement runs = 20 × PA/600.

Position adjustment per 162 starts: C +12.5, SS +7.5, 2B/3B/CF +2.5, LF/RF −7.5, 1B −12.5, DH −17.5. Starts approximate positional workload; individual defense is credited separately from the positional adjustment. Pitching WAR* = (league FIP* + 1.00 − pitcher FIP*) × IP/9/10. The +1.00 replacement allowance applies to both starters and relievers. League averages are weighted by PA/IP; trades retain separate team stints. No full-season extrapolation is used, so short seasons produce less accumulated WAR. Small samples remain unstable.

BsR* adds stolen-base runs, extra-base advancement and double-play avoidance. Def Runs* adds fielding plays/errors, double plays turned, throws limiting advancement and catcher steal control. For each recorded opportunity, runs = (observed successful outcome − neutral player success probability) × run weight. Neutral means rating 50 for the relevant runner or fielder, with the opposing player held fixed. Weights: ball-in-play out versus reach .75; extra out from a double play .45; first-to-third .20; scoring from second on a single or first on a double .40; productive-out advancement .20/.40; caught stealing versus successful steal .60. Values can be negative and include outcome variation.

Infield hits/triples stay in batting value; stolen-base value is counted once. Pitching WAR remains FIP-based, so it does not also award these fielding outcomes to pitchers. This is a simplified opportunity model, not DRS/UZR or a full run-expectancy model. Fielders are selected by the existing engine, not measured batted-ball difficulty. Historical events are not reconstructed from today's ratings.
